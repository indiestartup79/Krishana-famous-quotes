================================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: INDIA NEWS DIGEST (SEPTEMBER 2026)
================================================================================

1. GEOPOLITICS:
   • Hosted the 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi with heads of state from China, Russia, and Iran.
   • PM Modi warned against the weaponisation of technology and critical minerals.
   • India-China bilateral talks signaled stabilizing ties, while trade negotiations expanded with MERCOSUR.

2. FINANCE & ECONOMY:
   • Q1 FY27 real GDP growth recorded at a robust 7.8%, outpacing major peer economies.
   • August CPI inflation ticked up to 4.82% due to food and energy price pressures.
   • RBI maintained the benchmark repo rate at 5.25% with a neutral stance.
   • Markets saw volatility amid high crude prices, but IPO activity surged (notably NSE IPO).

3. TECHNOLOGY:
   • SEMICON India 2026 convened in New Delhi with 600+ exhibitors and 40+ countries as chip plants go operational.
   • Focus accelerated on sovereign compute, population-scale AI, and indigenous models (BHASHINI, Sarvam AI).
   • ISRO launched GSLV-F17/EOS-05; startups raised $412M+ in a single week, led by Pixxel's $100M Series C.

4. FILM & ENTERTAINMENT:
   • "Mirzapur: The Movie" made a major theatrical box-office splash starring Pankaj Tripathi and Ali Fazal.
   • Priyadarshan's "Haiwaan" brought together Akshay Kumar and Saif Ali Khan in an action face-off.
   • Strong theatrical footfall and multiple clashes across Hindi and regional film releases.

5. HEALTH & MEDICINE:
   • ICMR reported Phase 3 clinical trials of India's indigenous single-dose dengue vaccine across 13,000+ participants, targeting late 2027 delivery.
   • Draft National Health Research Policy 2026 proposed raising public health research funding to 0.15% of GDP.
   • Active monitoring for seasonal H1N1 influenza confirmed standard circulating strains without mutations; palliative care programs expanded nationwide.
